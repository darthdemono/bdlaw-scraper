# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- The Wilson reader cleans the image readings (Cyrillic and Greek look-alikes, LaTeX, line breaks, repeated phrases, scripts the book never uses), takes small-caps detection from the text layer, splits run-together entries with accented and variant headwords, folds detached sentence fragments back, reads full-name language codes such as `Hindi (` and `Uriya,`, and starts the index at page 653.
- `bdlaw-wilson-lines` reads every printed line of Wilson's glossary from the page images with surya (about 9 lines a second on a 6 GB GPU), which brings back the accents the scan's text layer loses (á read as d) and the Arabic, Devanagari, Bengali, Telugu and other words in Unicode. `bdlaw-wilson --lines FILE` builds the entries from those readings, falling back to the text layer for a line with none; native script in the text is wrapped in language-tagged spans.
- `bdlaw-wilson --output-table` writes the whole glossary as one note: a table of term, language and meaning under each letter, with `[[...#Letter]]` links for "See X" references, recognised native words as language-tagged spans, and a dashed mark on words whose consonants do not match the Latin spelling beside them. Junk words are found per word against the system English word list plus the book's own repeated words, entries are split by a per-column indentation boundary, headwords that ran together are split, and the OCR's `d` for `á` is allowed.
- `bdlaw-wilson-native` reads the native-script words of Wilson's glossary from page crops with surya, falling back to tesseract when the script is wrong for the entry's language code. `bdlaw-wilson` now stores each native word's page and box.
- Original clause numbering is kept: each list item carries its marker in a `<!--n:(a)-->` comment, and every act gets a `.numbering/act-N.json` sidecar file.
- Packaged as `bdlaw-scraper` with the commands `bdlaw-scraper`, `bdlaw-dictionaries`, `bdlaw-translate` and `bdlaw-pdf2md`.

- Dictionary pages (Law Dictionary, Glossary, Latin Terms) are written A to Z with one section and table per letter. Source citations, such as `[Wilson’s Glossary]` and case references in parentheses, are moved out of the definitions into footnotes. `bdlaw-dictionaries --reformat FILE -c NAME` re-renders an older table file offline.

- Acts named in dictionary footnotes ("the Penal Code, 1860") become `[[act-N|…]]` wikilinks, and a section named just before them links to that section's block anchor. This needs `bdlaw-dictionaries --acts-dir <folder of act-N.md>`; a name links only when exactly one act has it.
- Dictionary entries whose terms differ only in case or punctuation are merged. Identical or wholly contained definitions collapse; definitions that really differ are all kept in one entry.

- The dictionary pages carry a `## Footnotes` heading before their footnote definitions, as the acts do. The site drops it and prints its own footnote label.

- Act titles that end in a note ("…Act, 1950 (East Bengal Act)") now match footnote references to the plain name.

- `bdlaw-terms` builds a glossary of defined terms from the definitions inside the acts: one note per initial letter, the newest wording first as the current definition, earlier wordings kept with what changed, the same wording in several acts listed once, and definitions that only point at another act listed as references. English and Bengali terms are kept apart.

- The defined-terms pages say they are generated by a program, list the steps it follows and its limits, and carry `generated: true` in their frontmatter.

- `bdlaw-wilson` rebuilds the entries of Wilson's 1855 glossary from the scan's own text layer: page headers and numbers dropped, two columns in reading order, entry starts found by indentation, glossary, supplement and index told apart, native-script groups recorded as placeholders with their language code instead of garbled OCR.

### Fixed

- Cross-references to several sections at once ("sections 64, 65, 66 and 445") only linked the first number. Every known section in the list is now linked; a quantity after "and" ("sections 3 and 4 years") is left alone.
- Bengali references: "ধারা ৫(২)(ক)" and "ধারা ৫ এর উপ-ধারা (২) এর দফা (ক)" are one link to the section, number-first forms ("৫ ও ৬ ধারা") link their numbers, and "বিধি" and "প্রবিধান" count like "ধারা". "উপ-ধারা" and "উপধারা" are never taken for a section. English "section 5(2)(a)" links the same way.
- The preamble keeps the links to the acts it names and its sentences are no longer split at each link, and section references inside it are linked.
- Bengali text: a danda typed as "৷" (U+09F7) is written as "।", which also stops "২৪ক৷" turning into "২৪কি". Legacy-font artefacts ("পর্যন্ত্ম", "ত্মেগত্রে") are repaired, word-final "ত্" becomes "ৎ", and spacing around , ; : । is fixed.
- Bengali "ব্যাখ্যা" and "ব্যতিক্রম" become Explanation and Exception callouts (not inside a quoted amendment), and Bengali punishment phrases are bold as in English.
- "Firstly: ...", "Secondly: ..." paragraphs are lists too, and the list that follows an item (for example (1) and (2) under "Fourthly") nests under it. An inline "(a)" in the item opens that sub-list.
- "First.- ...", "Second. ..." paragraphs (section 21 of the Penal Code and similar) are now real lists with the word as the marker.
- Illustration lists inside callouts carried the section's running number ("5.") and rendered as plain text. Each callout list now starts at 1.
- Headings that link nowhere and hold a footnote reference showed as literal "[...](#)" (328 headings in 117 acts). They are plain headings now, and amendment brackets inside a title are dropped.
- A preamble ending in a lone dash turned its text into a heading.
- Footnote references were lost in many places, leaving about 600 notes without a reference: Bengali-digit brackets were read as references, a footnote number in front of a bracketed word in a heading ("Prohibited 18 [entity]") or glued to a chapter name was dropped, the preamble lost its references, and a closer before an adjacent reference ("335[1995]") ate the first reference's bracket. Notes now keep their references. A bracketed year with no matching note stays plain text.
- Editorial brackets that span several lines or sit open in front of a wikilink ("[[[act-354|..]]") are removed, and an amendment bracket around a sub-chapter heading no longer becomes a broken wikilink.
- Footnote numbers are de-duplicated, nested "[28[29]" references split into two, block ids are unique, and a "[Omitted](c)" inside a heading is escaped so it is not read as a link.
- Section lists in other forms: Bengali section numbers, a reference to another act is left unlinked, double-letter and suffix list markers ("(aa)", "(2A)"), and table rows are no longer split by list conversion.
- Frontmatter values are quoted as JSON and links to acts that do not exist point to the official site.
- A list that starts with a deep marker such as roman (i) straight after a paragraph was indented 6 spaces and shown as a code block (138 items in 92 acts). Items now start at the top level and nest only one level at a time.
- Acts with an Explanation callout followed by an Illustration or Exception failed with `NameError: _RE_SECONDARY_HDR` (121 acts).

### Removed

- The Pinecone upload scripts and the `.env` handling that only they used.
